EVENT POSTERS

VIVA POSTER


This poster shows summary figures of the four experimental chapters of my PhD thesis: I) showing how musico-acoustic features relate to memory qualities, II-III) how music-listening features relate to memory outcomes in a VR encoding paradigm and IV) how music-listening features relate to memory encoding and retrieval effects from an experience sampling paradigm. A representative way of summing up 3 years of PhD blood, sweat, tears and science into an A3 poster.


You might have to read my thesis to find out more ;)

SOFAR RDU BLACK HISTORY MONTH POSTER


I created this poster for Sofar RDU's Black History Month show in 2022, hosted at Black-owned bookshop Rofhiwa Book Cafe in Durham, North Carolina. Using Black History Month colours, it depicts a lively band playing a concert.

SOFAR STAMP POSTER


For this poster, I took scans from my father's 40+ year stamp collection and created a digital collage. All text and image on the poster is derived from digital copies of stamp materials.

SOFAR ANDY WARHOL POSTER


If you couldn't tell, this poster is heavily inspired by Andy Warhol's famous Campbell's soup cans paintings. Not sure if there's much else to say other than that. It was fun to make.

SOFAR NC TRIBUTE POSTER


This was the last poster I made for Sofar RDU before moving to London in 2019. It was a tribute to North Carolina's "Triangle" (referring to the areas encompassed between Raleigh, Durham and Chapel Hill), where I imagined what a functional public transportation system might look like (you're welcome, city planners). Perhaps it's a long shot but it was fun to dream of a better-connected reality for North Carolinians.

SOFAR BAUHAUS POSTER


This poster is inspired by the Bauhaus design tradition - I remember at the time, I had recently attended an exhibition celebrating 100 years of Bauhaus and I was obsessed. Its since become highly influential in my art and design style. One of my favorite posters.
